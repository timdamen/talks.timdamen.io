Tim Damen is a frontend developer and web accessibility specialist from Amsterdam, the Netherlands 🇳🇱. He is passionate about HTML, CSS, JavaScript, DX, web standards, accessibility, and developing robust web apps. Tim has over a decade of experience in the field and speaks at conferences and meetups around the world. He also hosts a podcast about digital accessibility called [focustrap](https://focusring.io/podcast-focustrap).
